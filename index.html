How to play
  • Each game is 5 rounds. Every round shows one photo of a famous moment.
  • Season, up to 1,000 points. Exact is 1,000, one year off about 700, three off about 300, ten or more off 0. Club seasons count by the year they ended (1998–99 = 1999). International matches use their calendar year.
  • Teams, 500 points per correct team, in either order.
  • Competition, 1,000 points if exact, or 400 for the right kind (e.g. another continental club cup).
  • The photo starts zoomed in. Zoom out or Reveal decade each cost 20% of that round's score.

Photos: Wikimedia Commons contributors and other sources. See manifest.json.
